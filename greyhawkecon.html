Mike Breault

Game Designer

Mike has been working in the game industry since 1984, starting out on D&D and AD&D games (among others) at TSR. After 5 years there, he caught the video game bug and worked his way into the video game industry while continuing freelance work for TSR and Milton Bradley for a few years. He's been teaching game design and narrative design at Webster University in St. Louis for the last 8 years. He returned to working on analog games in his spare time, with his first self-published game, Rubber Ducky Rampage!, completing a successful Kickstarter campaign in early June; two versions of the game will be available for purchase at the con.

Boardgamegeek.com has a listing of the games Mike's worked on over his career.

Mike will be doing demos his new game Rubber Ducky Rampage!

Command a fleet of brave little duckies, gather bubbles to power them up, and sink your rivals to the bottom of the Tub!

Brad Crowell

Game Designer - Gridmaster

Brad Crowell grew up playing board games with his younger brother and single mother, which was a time he learned to play chess. As a result, he desired to create games for others to enjoy.

As a father, he would play games with his two daughters. They combined all the decks for Uno, Uno Spin, and Uno Attack for a nice game of "Ultimate Uno".

As a grandfather of 7 now, he hopes to share his joy of board games with the newest generation. What better way is there than to leave a creative legacy?

Brad earns a living through self-employment in the gig work industry. This allows him the opportunity to earn money anywhere he travels to introduce Gridmaster (in development since 2023) at conventions and game clubs. He hopes to find a publisher when he attends a convention in Dallas this upcoming November, thus fulfilling a life-long dream.

Brad will be running his new prototype game. It hasn’t been named yet, but he will be gathering suggestions and getting feedback at the con.

As the owner of a mining company, you hope to win an exclusive contract with a prominent jewelry company. Your team has accepted the challenge to secure the necessary jewels the quickest. Beware fatigue while navigating through a crazy network of passages in mining the jewels. Will your team emerge from the mine, ready to secure the contract?
