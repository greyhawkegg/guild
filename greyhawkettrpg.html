Devil's Due
Pirate Borg, the Worst Pirate RPG Ever Made! SESSION 1 • 12:00–4:00 PM Up to 6 Scallywags, Reprobates, or Luckless Landlubbers

Adam Busenbark — Table 4

Pirate Borg is a rules light game with an older edition D&D feel, custom built to facilitate scurvy-ridden rum-soaked pirate adventures in a dark and twisted version of the Caribbean. Life is cheap, (un)death is prevalent, and new characters roll up rather quickly! Join me for a four hour sandbox session where you'll get to choose your own misadventure as you try to survive the debt you owe to an infamous pirate captain.

After nearly dying on your last voyage, you were saved by the infamous pirate captain Fredrick “Sea Devil” Black. The captain then unceremoniously dumped you onto the docks of an unfamiliar port. Nothing in the Dark Caribbean is free, and you instinctively know that Black will be back with high expectations. You have the four hours of our session to fix your situation. Go treasure hunting in hopes of paying off your debt, steal a ship and make a run for it, find a defensible location and dig in your heels for a fight. Do whatever you can, as fast as you can, as you've only got four hours, and this Devil always gets his due.

Everybody Dies at Midnight
DRAGONBANE SESSION 1 • 12:00–4:00 PM UP TO 6 PLAYERS

Bruce McCarthy — Table 5

Level/Requirements: No level requirement

Experience Required: None, completely beginner-friendly. I’ll teach the rules at the table.

You may use the Dragonbane character creation site to create characters quickly and jump right into the adventure: Dragonbane Character Creation Site

Adventure Synopsis:
Welcome to Lastwick, where tonight is the biggest celebration of the year.
The beer is free. The music is loud. The entire village is celebrating.
Because at midnight, everybody dies.
It has happened every year for 117 years. When the clock strikes twelve, every man, woman, and child in Lastwick drops dead—and when the sun rises, they wake up perfectly fine.
At least, that's what's supposed to happen.
This year, the clock strikes twelve and the villagers fall dead... but the adventurers don't.
Then the graves begin to open.
With dawn approaching and Lastwick's dead refusing to stay buried, the heroes must descend beneath the village, uncover the secret of the Last Night, and destroy the ancient evil that has been waiting 117 years for its chance to escape.
Tonight, everybody dies. The question is whether they'll wake up.

SESSION 24:15 – 8:15 PM
Souls for the Taking
RISING LEGENDS SESSION 2 • 4:15–8:15 PM 6 PLAYERS

Clint Menezes — Table 6

For months, your hometown has suffered under the threat of a ruthless bandit gang. Travelers have vanished on the roads, farms have been raided, and the townsfolk are afraid to venture beyond the safety of their homes. With the local guards stretched thin, the responsibility falls to a brave band of adventurers to track the bandits to their hideout and put an end to their attacks.

Follow the trail into the surrounding wilderness, uncover the bandits’ hidden camp, and face the criminals who have been terrorizing your home. But be warned — the bandits know the land, they have prepared defenses, and they may have more dangerous plans than simple robbery. Protect your friends, defend your hometown, and show these outlaws that your community will not be an easy target.

Delve into a deep and unique fantasy world to explore and fight for survival. This all d12 tabletop roleplaying game focuses on heavy character customization and teamwork. Rising Legends allows you to watch your character leave their mark on history.

You may build your character online at GrinningDemonGames.com or we offer prebuilt characters.

Van Richton’s Guide to Ravenloft: in Valachan the Domain of the Hunter
DND 5E SESSION 2 • 4:15–8:15 PM 6 PLAYERS

A rag tag group of six individuals are pulled from their world to participate in a hunt. As the Prey. In order for this world to survive, blood must be spilled in a ceremonial hunt to appease the hunger of the land. A jungle filled with danger and beauty.
